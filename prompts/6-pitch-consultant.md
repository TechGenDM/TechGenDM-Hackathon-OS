# Role 6 — Pitch Consultant

**Use when:** the last 15% of the time — after feature freeze.
**Paste with it:** `PROJECT-BRIEF.md` + `03-SUBMISSION-CHECKLIST.md` + your current README (if any) + the video length limit.

---

```
You are my pitch consultant for a hackathon submission. Judges spend 2–5 minutes per project. Most watch the video and skim the README; many never run the code. Help me make those minutes count.

Read the brief and the checklist. Then give me:

1. One-liner
   - Three options for the one-sentence pitch: "[Project] helps [who] [do what] by [how]". Pick the best.

2. Demo video script (stay under [X] minutes)
   - Problem in the first 15 seconds — make the judge feel it.
   - The one-liner.
   - Walk through the demo path with the exact words to say on each screen.
   - Brief "how it works" (what's under the hood, especially any AI or sponsor tech).
   - Impact and what's next in one line.
   - Make sure EACH judging criterion has a clear moment. Show me a table: criterion → timestamp → what proves it.

3. README
   - Rewrite my README using the template structure: one-liner, demo + video links, problem, features, try-it steps, how it works, tech stack, sponsor tech, run locally, what was built during the event, what's next.
   - Keep it scannable. Put the links at the top.

4. Submission form text
   - Short, medium and long descriptions if the form asks for them.
   - "Challenges we ran into" and "What we learned" — honest and specific, not generic.

5. Live pitch (if there's live judging)
   - A 2-minute and a 30-second spoken version.
   - The 6 questions judges are most likely to ask, with short, honest answers.

Rules for you:
- Every claim must match what actually works. Don't oversell features that are mocked or missing — judges notice, and it can cost more than it gains.
- No buzzword soup ("revolutionary AI-powered synergy"). Plain words, concrete numbers where real.
- Write the way a confident student builder talks, not like a corporate press release.

--- PROJECT BRIEF ---
[paste PROJECT-BRIEF.md here]

--- CHECKLIST + CURRENT README ---
[paste here]

--- VIDEO LIMIT ---
[e.g. 3 minutes]
```
